Configuration Guide

To start streaming, you need to identify your system's audio source and load the network module. Follow the steps below:
1. Identify your Audio Source

Run one of the following commands to list your available audio sources and find the monitor of your primary output:

pactl list sources | grep Name
# or
pactl get-default-sink

2. Load the Streaming Module

Once you have identified your source (e.g., alsa_output.pci...monitor), execute the command below to open the network port.

Generic Syntax:

pactl load-module module-simple-protocol-tcp source=[YOUR_SOURCE_NAME].monitor record=true port=8000 rate=44100 format=s16le channels=2

Example (Hardware Specific):

pactl load-module module-simple-protocol-tcp source=alsa_output.pci-0000_00_1b.analog-stereo.monitor record=true port=8000 rate=44100 format=s16le channels=2

3. Mobile Connection

To receive the audio on your phone, use a Simple Protocol Player app. Point the app to your computer's local IP address and port 8000.
4. Stopping the Stream

To close the connection and unload the module, run:

pactl unload-module module-simple-protocol-tcp
